Six-month internship in Automation & Robotics with Vrindabhumi Enterprise LLP, Kota, in collaboration with the Research Designs and Standards Organisation (RDSO) under the Ministry of Railways. Assisted in the installation and calibration of thermal imaging cameras, GPS mapping of overhead-equipment poles, and field inspections for system compliance — supporting real-time monitoring, data collection and reporting on temperature variation and OHE height-stagger measurements. The project extends to automated pantograph hotspot and wear detection: edge-AI thermal imaging and machine vision applied to in-service locomotive pantographs to flag hotspots, carbon-strip wear and anomalies for predictive maintenance.
